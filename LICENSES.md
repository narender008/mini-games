## Assets

Balloon Pop uses no image, model or sound files from anywhere else. Every texture (sky, clouds, sea ripples, fabric weave, wicker, balloon patterns), every model (balloon envelopes, baskets, pin, dart, birds) and every sound (pops, splashes, wind and water) is generated in code at runtime. The cover picture `games/balloon-pop/cover.jpg` and the icons are screenshots and drawings of this project's own work.

The balloon shapes and colours were designed after a reference photograph of a hot-air balloon at dusk. The photograph itself is not part of this repository and is not used as a texture.
