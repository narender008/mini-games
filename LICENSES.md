## Assets

Balloon Pop uses no image, model or sound files from anywhere else. Every texture (sky, clouds, sea ripples, fabric weave, wicker, balloon patterns, walnut and hazel wood grain), every model (balloon envelopes, baskets, pin, dart, air rifle, slingshot, stones, birds) and every sound (pops, splashes, wind and water, rifle shot and cocking, slingshot draw and release, chimes, fanfares) is generated in code at runtime. The cover picture `games/balloon-pop/cover.jpg` and the icons are screenshots and drawings of this project's own work.

The balloon shapes and colours were designed after a reference photograph of a hot-air balloon at dusk. The photograph itself is not part of this repository and is not used as a texture.

Cake Cut likewise uses no outside image, model or sound files. The cakes (sponge crumb, creams, jams, ganache, fondant, drips, fruit and every other filling and coating), the linen tablecloth, the porcelain stand and plates, the room, the fairy lights, the knives, cake wire, cake sword and server, and every sound (cutting, crumbs, clinks, the match, candle breath and puffs, the Happy Birthday melody, confetti, chimes, the slice pop and the cheer) are generated in code at runtime. The Happy Birthday melody is the traditional tune, which is in the public domain. The cover picture `games/cake-cut/cover.jpg` and the icons are screenshots and drawings of this project's own work.

Rocket Blast also uses no image, model or sound files from anywhere else. Every model (the rocket and its pilot, the toy blocks, aliens and robots, the boss, the weapons, stars, gems, sweets and confetti, planets, hills, flowers and butterflies), every texture (skies, clouds, nebulae, the sprite atlas, sweet stripes, noise) and every sound (music, blasts, chimes, boops and giggles, fanfares, fireworks) is generated in code at runtime. The cover picture `games/rocket-blast/cover.jpg` and the icons are screenshots and drawings of this project's own work. The game was designed from a written brief and two mock-up pictures; the pictures are not part of this repository.

Garden Grow uses no image, model or sound files from anywhere else either. The sky, sun, moon, clouds and stars, the soil with its crumbs, clods and pebbles, the lawn, gravel and decking, every prop (the timber edging, stepping stones, terracotta pots, picket and panel fences, raised planters, the balcony, the hedges and trees behind, the basket and the glass vase), every flower, vegetable and leaf, every visitor (butterflies, bees, ladybirds, birds, fireflies and moths), the watering can, water, rain and rainbow, and every sound (soil, water, rain, leaves, birdsong, chimes and the music) are generated in code at runtime. The bedtime music is an original lullaby and the traditional tune Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star, which is in the public domain. The cover picture `games/garden-grow/cover.jpg` and the icons are screenshots and drawings of this project's own work.

Block Tower also uses no image, model or sound files from anywhere else. Every block (the cubes, bricks, planks, pillars, cylinders, arches, roofs and half-rounds, their maple, beech and walnut grain, lacquered paint and letter faces), every room (floorboards, rug, decking, walls, windows, garden and furniture), every knock-down toy (the ball, the wind-up car and the wrecking ball) and every sound (wooden clacks, rolling, music, chimes and sparkles) is generated in code at runtime. The cover picture `games/block-tower/cover.jpg` and the icons are screenshots and drawings of this project's own work.

Marble Run also uses no image, model or sound files from anywhere else. Every marble (clear, swirled, cat's-eye, glowing and planet marbles), every track piece (wooden troughs, painted and lacquered blocks, clear tubes, funnels, loops, wheels, bells, flippers, lifts), every material (wood grain, paint wear, acrylic, glowing glass, crystal, brass, gold, chrome, ceramic, stone, slate and asteroid rock), every setting (the sunlit playroom, the crystal night, the storybook castle with its friendly dragon and the cosmic observatory) and every sound (rolling, clicks, bells, whooshes, the lifts, chimes and the music) is generated in code at runtime. The cover picture `games/marble-run/cover.jpg`, the level pictures in `games/marble-run/previews/` and the icons are screenshots and drawings of this project's own work. Every boat (the speedboat, the wooden sailboat and its sail, the rubber-duck boat and the jet ski), every place (the lagoon's palms, sand, coral and volcanic island, the lake's pines, jetty, boathouse and mountains, the canal town's houses, quays, bridges and boats, and the night bay's village, lighthouse, lanterns and fireflies), every animal (dolphins, ducks, turtles, fish and birds), the water, wake, spray, sky, sun, moon and stars, and every sound (motors, water, splashes, horns, chimes, animal calls, nature sounds and the music) is generated in code at runtime. Every friend (the Paint Pals, the butterfly, the flower, tree, rainbow and sun, the car, rocket, boat and teddy, and the bunny, kitten, puppy, penguin and turtle), their fur, scales, skin, eyes and materials, the paint itself (acrylic, watercolour, glitter and the canvas weave), the easel and brushes, the treehouse studio and garden with its trees, flowers, pond, waterfall, lanterns and lights, the sky, the garden's songbirds, butterflies, dragonflies, frog and koi, the toys (bubbles, ball, star-berry, hearts, confetti and paint puddles), and every sound (brush strokes, water, glitter, the magic, the friends' calls, the toys, birdsong, breeze and the music) is generated in code at runtime. Toy Tanks is the first game here with photographed assets. Its skies and lighting (five HDRIs) and its ground and prop textures (grass, soil, mossy rock, sand, damp sand, mud, rock, planks, pine bark, moss, snow and rope) are made from CC0 originals from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) and [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/), cropped, regraded, resized and re-encoded into `games/toy-tanks/assets/env/` and `games/toy-tanks/assets/tex/`. Each original, its author, download URL, date and checksums are listed in `games/toy-tanks/assets/SOURCES.md`. CC0 needs no attribution; the authors are credited there anyway. Everything else is this project's own: the six toy tank models and the props (sandcastle, bucket, spade, shells, watering can, fence, rope bridge and the rest) are built by the Blender 5.2 scripts in `games/toy-tanks/blender/` and compressed with [gltfpack](https://github.com/zeux/meshoptimizer) 1.3.0 into `games/toy-tanks/assets/models/` (no `.blend` files are kept); the terrain, lakes, sea and streams, trees, grass, flowers, rocks and weather, every burst, splat and crater, and every sound and the music are generated in code at runtime. The cover picture `games/toy-tanks/cover.jpg`, the stage pictures in `games/toy-tanks/previews/` and the icons are screenshots and drawings of this project's own work. The game was designed after reference pictures; the pictures are not part of this repository and are not used as textures.

Iron Barrage uses no image, model or sound files from anywhere else. The tanks, their crews, the ordnance, debris and gore, the jet, the parachute and the battlefield props are modelled by the Blender 5.2 scripts in `games/iron-barrage/blender/` and pre-rendered into the sprite atlas and normal map in `games/iron-barrage/assets/sprites/` (no `.blend` files are kept); the ground, skies, backdrops, weather, every explosion, fire, smoke and blood effect, and every sound and the music are generated in code at runtime. The cover picture `games/iron-barrage/cover.jpg` and the icons are screenshots and drawings of this project's own work.

The look was designed after reference pictures supplied for the design, which are not part of this repository. The cover picture `games/horde-buster/cover.jpg` and the icons are screenshots and drawings of this project's own work.
