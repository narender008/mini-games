## Assets

Balloon Pop uses no image, model or sound files from anywhere else. Every texture (sky, clouds, sea ripples, fabric weave, wicker, balloon patterns, walnut and hazel wood grain), every model (balloon envelopes, baskets, pin, dart, air rifle, slingshot, stones, birds) and every sound (pops, splashes, wind and water, rifle shot and cocking, slingshot draw and release, chimes, fanfares) is generated in code at runtime. The cover picture `games/balloon-pop/cover.jpg` and the icons are screenshots and drawings of this project's own work.

The balloon shapes and colours were designed after a reference photograph of a hot-air balloon at dusk. The photograph itself is not part of this repository and is not used as a texture.

Cake Cut likewise uses no outside image, model or sound files. The cakes (sponge crumb, creams, jams, ganache, fondant, drips, fruit and every other filling and coating), the linen tablecloth, the porcelain stand and plates, the room, the fairy lights, the knives, cake wire, cake sword and server, and every sound (cutting, crumbs, clinks, the match, candle breath and puffs, the Happy Birthday melody, confetti, chimes, the slice pop and the cheer) are generated in code at runtime. The Happy Birthday melody is the traditional tune, which is in the public domain. The cover picture `games/cake-cut/cover.jpg` and the icons are screenshots and drawings of this project's own work.
